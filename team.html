Why I’m building this

I’ve seen leaks firsthand. Then I saw one move a data center.

Visiting Cal Poly’s new AI data center, I learned the previous one had flooded from a water-cooling leak, and the team had to move into a new facility.

I spent 15+ years building data center infrastructure at Microsoft, Oracle and IBM. As AI racks go liquid-cooled, every rack becomes a plumbing problem.

MayimFlow exists so operators see the warning signs before the floor gets wet.
